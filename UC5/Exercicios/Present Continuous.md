Lembrando a estrutura:

> **Subject + verb to be + verb-ing**

**Ex.:** She is studying.

---

**Study**

I am **studying**.
I am not **studying**.
Am I **studying**?

**Work**

He is **working** today.
He isn't **working** today.
Is he **working** today?

**Read**

She's **reading** her book on the stairs.
She is not **reading** her book on the stairs.
Is she **reading** her book on the stairs?

**Write**

They are **writing** their book and sleeping on the chair, come see!
They aren't **writing** their book and sleeping on the chair.
Are they **writing** their book and sleeping in the chair?

**Speak**

Just shut your mouth while adults are **speaking** and wait for them to stop.
Don't shut your mouth while adults are **speaking**, just spit it out.
Do I have to shut my mouth while adults are **speaking** and wait fot them to stop?

**Cook**

You are **cooking** for me.
You are not **cooking** for me.
Are you **cooking** for me?

**Clean**

She is **cleaning** the entire house.
She is not **cleaning** the entire house.
Is she **cleaning** the entire house?

**Watch**

My dad is **watching** TV now.
My dad isn't **watching** TV now.
Is my dad **watching** TV now?

**Play**

You are **playing** now.
You're not **playing** now.
Are you **playing** now?

**Sleep**

She's **sleeping**, don't be loud.
She is not **sleeping**, be loud.
Is she **sleeping**?

### Passe as frases em Simple Present para o Present Continuous

1. She **drinks** coffee in the morning.
2. They **run** in the park.
3. He **drives** to work.
4. I **listen** to music.
5. We **make** dinner together.
6. John **swims** in the pool.
7. My mother **buys** vegetables at the market.
8. The students **learn** English.
9. Anna **takes** pictures of her friends.
10. The dog **sits** near the door.

**Por exemplo:**

**Simple Present:** She drinks coffee in the morning.
**Present Continuous:** She is drinking coffee now.
