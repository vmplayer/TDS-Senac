### 1. Descubra o erro

Todas as frases abaixo têm **um erro** no Simple Present. Encontre o erro e reescreva a frase corretamente.

a) She *work* at a hospital.

She **workes** at a hospital.

b) They *likes* pizza.

They **like** pizza.

c) He *study* English every day.

He **studies** English every day.

d) We *goes* to school by bus.

We **go** to school by bus.

e) My brother *watch* TV at night.

My brother **watches** TV at night

f) I *likes* chocolate.
    
I **like** chocolate.

### 2. Complete e descubra a regra

Complete as frases com a forma correta do verbo entre parênteses.

a) I **study** English every day. (study)

b) She **studies** English every day. (study)

c) They **go** to school by bus. (go)

d) He **goes** to school by bus. (go)

e) We **watch** TV at night. (watch)

f) My sister **watches** TV at night. (watch)

g) You **drink** coffee in the morning. (drink)

h) My father **drinks** coffee in the morning. (drink)

**Agora pense:** O que muda quando o sujeito é **he, she ou it**?

### 3. Quem é essa pessoa?

Leia as informações e descubra **quem pode ser a pessoa**. Depois, escreva frases completas usando Simple Present.

**Person A**

* works at a school
* teaches English
* reads many books
* goes to work by bus
* likes coffee

**Person B**

* works at a hospital
* helps sick people
* studies at night
* drinks a lot of water
* doesn't like coffee

**Person C**

* works in an office
* uses a computer
* goes to work by car
* enjoys music
* doesn't work on Sundays

Agora responda:

a) Who works at a school? Person A

b) Who studies at night? Person B

c) Who uses a computer? Person C

d) Who doesn't like coffee? Person B

e) Who enjoys music? Person C

### 4. Pergunte para descobrir

Imagine que você está tentando descobrir a rotina de um colega.

Crie perguntas usando as palavras abaixo.

**Exemplo:**
you / work / on Saturdays?
→ Do you work on Saturdays?

Agora faça:

a) you / like / pizza?

→ Do you like pizza?

b) she / study / English?

→ Does she study English?

c) he / play / soccer?

→ Does he play soccer?

d) they / go / to school by bus?

→ Do they go to school by bus?

e) your mother / work / on Sundays?

→ Do you mother work on Sundays?

f) your friends / watch / TV at night?

→ Do your friends watch TV at night?

### 5. Verdadeiro ou falso?

Leia as frases. Algumas são **gramaticalmente corretas**, algumas são **incorretas**.

Marque:

**C = correta**
**I = incorreta**


a) She works at a bank. C

b) He work at a bank. I

c) They likes music. I

d) I study English. C

e) My sister studies English. C

f) We goes to school every day. I

g) Pedro plays soccer on Saturdays. C

h) She don't like coffee. I

i) He doesn't like coffee. C

j) They don't work on Sundays. C
