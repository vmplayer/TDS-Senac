# Simple Present

## Exemplos de frases no simple present (presente simples)

| Exemplo                                               | Tradução                               |
| :---------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------- |
| 1 - I work everyday                                   | Eu trabalho todos os dias.             |
| 2 - She studies English at school.                    | Ele estuda inglês na escola.           |
| 3 - He plays soccer on weekends.                      | Ele joga futebol nos finais de semana. |
| 4 - We live in Brazil.                                | Nós moramos no Brasil.                 |
| 5 - They watch TV at night.                           | Eles assistem TV à noite.              |
| 6 - My mother cooks very well.                        | Minha mãe cozinha muito bem.           |
| 7 - John goes to school by bus.                       | John vai para a escola de ônibus.      |
| 8 - I like chocolate.                                 | Eu gosto de chocolate.                 |
| 9 - The dog sleeps in the house.                      | O cachorro dorme em casa.              |
| 10 - You speak English very well.                     | Você fala inglês muito bem.            |

1. Crie frases para os verbos listados abaixo. Faça, para o mesmo verbo, uma frase com I, You, We ou They E, AO LADO, a mesma frase com He, She ou It.

Work

We **work** at the office. | She **works** at the office.

Read

I **read** today. | He **reads** today.

Open

You **open** the window. | She **opens** the window.

Enjoy

You **enjoy**, if you want. | He **enjoys**, if he wants.

Study

I **study** so much. | She **studies** so much.

Carry

You **carry** the horse to the garden. | He **carries** the horse to the garden.

Catch 

If you **catch** him, I'll give you money. | If she **catches** him, I'll give her money.

Push

We **push** the curtain down. | He **pushes** the curtain down.

Mix 

They **mix** the drink. | He **mixes** the drink. 

Miss 

I **miss** you. | She **misses** you.

Go

I **go** on. | She **goes** on.
