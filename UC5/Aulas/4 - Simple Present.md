# Simple Present (Presente Simples)

## 1. O que é o Simple Present?

O **Simple Present** é usado principalmente para falar sobre:

* hábitos;
* rotinas;
* fatos;
* verdades gerais;
* situações que acontecem com frequência;
* situações relativamente permanentes.

### Exemplos:

* I work every day.
* She studies at night.
* They live in Brazil.
* The sun rises in the east.
* He works with technology.

---

## 2. Estrutura básica

### Frase afirmativa

A estrutura básica é:

**Sujeito + verbo**

| Sujeito | Verbo | Exemplo           |
| ------- | ----- | ----------------- |
| I       | work  | I work every day. |
| You     | study | You study a lot.  |
| We      | play  | We play soccer.   |
| They    | eat   | They eat pizza.   |

---

## 3. Regra do S com HE, SHE e IT

Quando o sujeito é:

* He
* She
* It

o verbo normalmente recebe **S**.

### Exemplos:

* He works.
* She studies.
* It runs.

---

## 4. Regras especiais da terceira pessoa

### Verbos terminados em:

* s
* sh
* ch
* x
* o

normalmente recebem **ES**.

### Exemplos:

* watch → watches
* go → goes
* fix → fixes
* wash → washes

Exemplos em frases:

* He watches TV.
* She goes to school.
* He fixes computers.

---

### Verbos terminados em consoante + Y

Troca-se o **Y** por **IES**.

### Exemplos:

* study → studies
* try → tries
* carry → carries

---

### Verbos terminados em vogal + Y

Nesse caso, apenas adicionamos **S**.

### Exemplos:

* play → plays
* enjoy → enjoys
* buy → buys

---

## 5. Caso especial: HAVE

O verbo **have** muda para **has** com:

* he;
* she;
* it.

### Exemplos:

* I have a computer.
* They have a class today.
* She has a computer.
* He has a new phone.

---

## 6. Frases negativas

Para formar frases negativas com verbos comuns, usamos:

**Sujeito + do/does + not + verbo na forma base**

### DO NOT / DON'T

Usamos com:

* I
* You
* We
* They

### DOES NOT / DOESN'T

Usamos com:

* He
* She
* It

| Sujeito | Auxiliar | Exemplo                    |
| ------- | -------- | -------------------------- |
| I       | don't    | I don't work on Sundays.   |
| You     | don't    | You don't study at night.  |
| We      | don't    | We don't play soccer.      |
| They    | don't    | They don't eat meat.       |
| He      | doesn't  | He doesn't work here.      |
| She     | doesn't  | She doesn't study English. |
| It      | doesn't  | It doesn't work.           |

---

## 7. Regra importante com DOES

Quando usamos **does** ou **doesn't**, o verbo principal volta para a sua forma base.

### Compare:

* She studies English.
* She doesn't study English.
* Does she study English?

### Incorreto:

* ❌ She doesn't studies English.
* ❌ Does she studies English?

### Correto:

* ✔ She doesn't study English.
* ✔ Does she study English?

O **S** já está representado pelo auxiliar **does**.

---

## 8. Frases interrogativas

Para fazer perguntas, usamos:

**Do/Does + sujeito + verbo na forma base?**

### Com DO:

* Do you work?
* Do they study English?
* Do we have class today?

### Com DOES:

* Does she work?
* Does he study English?
* Does it work?

---

## 9. Respostas curtas

### Respostas positivas

* Yes, I do.
* Yes, you do.
* Yes, we do.
* Yes, they do.
* Yes, he does.
* Yes, she does.

### Respostas negativas

* No, I don't.
* No, you don't.
* No, we don't.
* No, they don't.
* No, he doesn't.
* No, she doesn't.

### Exemplo:

**Does she study English?**

* Yes, she does.
* No, she doesn't.

---

## 10. Perguntas com palavras interrogativas

Também podemos fazer perguntas usando palavras como:

| Inglês | Significado  |
| ------ | ------------ |
| What   | o que / qual |
| Where  | onde         |
| When   | quando       |
| Why    | por quê      |
| Who    | quem         |
| How    | como         |

A estrutura mais comum é:

**Palavra interrogativa + do/does + sujeito + verbo?**

### Exemplos:

* Where do you work?
* What do you study?
* When do they play?
* Why do you study English?
* Where does she live?
* What does he do?
* How does this program work?

---

## 11. Advérbios de frequência

Os advérbios de frequência mostram com que frequência alguma coisa acontece.

### Alguns exemplos:

* Always = sempre
* Usually = geralmente
* Often = frequentemente
* Sometimes = às vezes
* Rarely = raramente
* Never = nunca

Com verbos comuns, eles normalmente aparecem **antes do verbo principal**.

### Estrutura:

**Sujeito + advérbio + verbo**

### Exemplos:

* I always study.
* She usually works at night.
* They often play games.
* We sometimes study together.
* He never drinks coffee.

---

## 12. Expressões de frequência

Também podemos indicar frequência utilizando expressões como:

* every day = todos os dias;
* every week = toda semana;
* every month = todo mês;
* every year = todo ano;
* once a week = uma vez por semana;
* twice a week = duas vezes por semana;
* three times a month = três vezes por mês;
* on Mondays = nas segundas-feiras.

Essas expressões normalmente aparecem no final da frase.

### Exemplos:

* I study English every day.
* She works on Mondays.
* We have classes twice a week.
* They play soccer every Saturday.

---

## 13. Exemplos do dia a dia

### Rotina

* I wake up at 7 a.m.
* I work every day.
* I drink coffee in the morning.
* She studies at night.
* They go to school in the afternoon.

### Tecnologia

* I work with technology.
* I use a computer every day.
* She develops websites.
* He studies programming.
* They use GitHub.
* The system stores user information.
* The application connects to a database.

---

## 14. Situações permanentes ou duradouras

O Simple Present também pode representar situações que permanecem verdadeiras por bastante tempo.

### Exemplos:

* I live in Brazil.
* She works at a software company.
* He speaks English.
* They study programming.
* We use JavaScript in class.

---

## 15. Horários e programações

O Simple Present também pode ser usado para horários e eventos programados.

### Exemplos:

* The class starts at 7 p.m.
* The bus leaves at 8 a.m.
* The store opens at 9 a.m.
* The meeting starts at 2 p.m.
